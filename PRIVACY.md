# Privacy Policy — GymBroFitness

_Last updated: 2026-09-26_

GymBroFitness is a local-first workout tracker. This page explains what data the app handles, why, and what control you have over it.

## Data stored on your device

Your training profile, program, workout history, personal records, and settings are stored locally on your device (in a local SQLite database) and are never sent anywhere by default. You can export or delete this data at any time from Settings, and uninstalling the app removes it entirely.

## Camera (Form Check)

The optional Form Check feature uses your device's camera to analyze exercise form in real time. Video frames are processed entirely on your device to estimate body position and rep quality — they are never recorded, stored, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere, including to us. Nothing from the camera leaves your phone.

## Optional account and cloud sync

Signing in is entirely optional — GymBroFitness works fully without an account. If you choose to create an account (email and password), we use this only to:

- Authenticate you, via our backend provider, Supabase.
- Back up and restore your workout data across devices, at your request ("Sync now") or automatically right after you sign in.

If you sign in, we store your email address and the same workout/profile data described above (as a single backup snapshot) in our Supabase project, scoped so that only your own signed-in account can read or write it. We do not sell, share, or use this data for advertising, and we do not run any third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in this app.
